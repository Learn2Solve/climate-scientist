# Kaplan et al. 2015 — SHIPS‑RII verification + predictability

**Citation**
- Kaplan, J., DeMaria, M., Rozoff, C. M., Knaff, J. A., Sampson, C. R., & Cione, J. J. (2015).
  *Evaluating Environmental Impacts on Tropical Cyclone Rapid Intensification Predictability Utilizing Statistical Models*.
  Weather and Forecasting, 30(5), 1374–1396. DOI: 10.1175/WAF-D-15-0032.1

**What it is**
- Verification and predictability analysis for RI statistical models across multiple lead times.

**Key points (from repository description)**
- Builds separate models for each lead time from 0–24 h.
- Combines the models into a consensus probability; verifies skill for RI prediction.

**Why we care**
- Guides how to report RI skill fairly and motivates consensus/ensemble baselines.
