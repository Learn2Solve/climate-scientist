# Kaplan et al. 2010 — Revised SHIPS Rapid Intensification Index (RII)

**Citation**
- Kaplan, J., DeMaria, M., & Knaff, J. A. (2010). *A revised tropical cyclone rapid intensification index for the Atlantic and eastern North Pacific basins*. Weather and Forecasting, 25(1), 220–241. DOI: 10.1175/2009WAF2222280.1

**What it is**
- Revised SHIPS-based Rapid Intensification Index (RII) using linear discriminant analysis.

**RI definition**
- Multiple thresholds (25, 30, 35 kt over 24 h).

**Key points (from abstract-level sources)**
- Uses large-scale SHIPS predictors to estimate RI probability over 24 h.
- Developed for Atlantic + eastern North Pacific basins.

**Why we care**
- This is the canonical statistical baseline; our `ri_logit_baseline.py` is a minimal, auditable analogue.
