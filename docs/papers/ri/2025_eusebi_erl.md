# Eusebi et al. 2025 — Sea surface salinity (SSS) improves RI forecasting

**Citation**
- Eusebi, C., Liang, C., & Zhu, L. (2025).
  *Sea surface salinity improves tropical cyclone rapid intensification forecasting*.
  Environmental Research Letters, 20(3), 034010. DOI: 10.1088/1748-9326/adac7f

**What it is**
- Adds sea surface salinity (SSS) to RI prediction models; evaluates benefit vs traditional predictors.

**Key points (from publisher summary)**
- SSS provides additional predictive signal beyond standard atmosphere/ocean variables.
- Demonstrates improved RI prediction skill when SSS is included.

**Why we care**
- Suggests missing surface‑ocean information may be a major RI driver; a target for future features.
