# Wang et al. 2025 — Contrastive learning for RI forecasting

**Citation**
- Wang, Y., Zheng, X., Qiu, X., & Yang, Y. (2025).
  *Contrastive learning model for tropical cyclone rapid intensification forecasting*.
  PNAS, 122(4), e2415501122. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2415501122

**What it is**
- Contrastive learning to improve RI prediction and reduce false alarms.

**Key points (from abstract on PMC)**
- RI defined as ≥13 m/s over 24 h.
- Dataset: 1,149 24‑h periods for NW Pacific TCs (2020–2021).
- Reports POD 92.3% and FAR 8.9% on their evaluation set.

**Why we care**
- Shows modern representation learning can materially improve RI detection.
